DUSTLINE

A courier. A desert highway city. Six jobs, five stars, ten lost relics.

Drive and walk the sun-baked streets of Dustline as Rae Calloway. Take jobs across the city, outrun the law, and find what the desert buried.

Paused

The desert waits. WASD to move, E to enter a car or start a job, Esc to keep playing.

How to play

Move: WASD walks and drives. Hold Shift to sprint on foot. Space brakes the car.

Cars: press E near any car to take it. Press E again to get out.

Fight: aim with the mouse, click to shoot, wheel or keys 1-2-3 to swap between fists, pistol and SMG. R reloads.

Jobs: drive to a glowing marker and press E to start the job. Finish all six.

Heat: the law comes in five levels. Lose them at the Desert Rose paint shop while driving, or lie low at Rae's garage on foot.

Relics: ten turquoise relics hide around the city. Each one pays.

Save: progress saves automatically. M toggles the music.
